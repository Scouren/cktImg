# Existing overlapping parent arrangements

AG364 already places its two output branches around the input and cascode channels. In the native drawing the outer channel columns are 270 and 530, surrounding inner columns 360 and 440. In Schemify the corresponding values are 450 and 830 around 560 and 720. Each inner column holds four successive transistors. Each outer column holds a PMOS load and its NMOS partner. The two parents' vertical extents overlap. The PMOS and NMOS devices remain distinct and retain their original ordered bindings. [AG364 published drawing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xz-group/AnalogGenie/efc25358939c6bedd247f28d3df61066964f3a90/Dataset/364/Book364.png)

AG1016 has five disjoint device memberships. Its eight-device output core overlaps the physical extents of all four other groups. The M17/M18/M23/M24 group also overlaps the M19/M20/M21/M22 and M7/M8 groups. The M19/M20/M21/M22 group also overlaps M5/M6. All seven source intersections and the three remaining nonintersections already appear in both native and actual Schemify exports. For each group, the box spans the physical ends of its own drain and source leads. A positive area of intersection requires extent on both axes. [AG1016 published drawing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xz-group/AnalogGenie/efc25358939c6bedd247f28d3df61066964f3a90/Dataset/1016/Book1016.png)

The regression compares the exact group-pair identities. Moving one whole parent into a disjoint room fails the check for both circuits. Actual Schemify re-export preserves all 13 AG364 devices and all 20 AG1016 devices, including their ordered MOS terminals, models and parameters. These two fixtures provide a boundary for automatic stage separation. [Interleaving regression](../../../tests/interleaved_parents.py)

# Evidence coverage for the “unmeasured” and “book” rules

The frozen inventory contains 497 current claims and five retired IDs. The map below locates all 497. The individual review covers all **63 tagged `unmeasured` and 18 tagged `book`**. Each of those IDs occurs once in the decision tables. The other 416 entries have different tags, including explicit design choices and invariants, and retain their earlier evidence status. The tags describe the basis the original author gave. A drawing can establish that an arrangement occurs. A frequency claim requires an appropriate sample of independent drawings. The current source headers retain the same set of unmeasured IDs. [Frozen inventory](image-rules/current-rule-inventory.json) · [Full text of the claims](image-rules/current-human-rules.md)

**Narrow** retains an observation for the stated circuit or rejects a stronger claim. **Policy** describes a caller, renderer or search decision whose correctness depends on the specified behavior. **Open** identifies a drawing preference for which the available evidence leaves competing explanations. The [new comparison](more-human-layout-patterns.md) supplies the cited mirror, gate, rail and passive-filter cases. The [earlier comparison](human-layout-patterns.md) covers the divider, amplifier feedback and comparator cases.

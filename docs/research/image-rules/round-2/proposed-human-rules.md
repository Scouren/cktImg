# Proposed human rules after the renewed image research

These are conditional rules to implement and test, not a claim that the
current engine follows them. Every entry points to source examples and
contrary cases. The current 84 rule headers remain preserved separately;
viewing the full corpus does not certify every historical frequency or
every existing selector.

The governing order is **identity → caller authority → candidate facts →
ownership → presentation and arrangement → actual frames → wires → final
validation**. Presentation capability and raw membership can inform one
another's admission, but neither may rewrite the netlist. Collect every raw
competitor before eligibility checks within the same ownership question.
Independent compatible groups do not become ambiguous merely because there
is more than one on a page.

Nine-port support reaches every index consumer. `analogOutput` and
`bias_neighbors` currently contain three-bit amplifier indices. The proposed
contract is incomplete until an output, bias and each other role can occupy
index eight safely. A wider recognizer without those changes is not an
acceptable partial implementation.

[The digital follow-up](sixth-symbols.md) found all 56 conventional gate
instances already recognized. It proposes exact graphical side defaults for
four intentional PFD/DAC boxes, preserving their box shape and electrical
direction semantics. Canonical sides and whole-symbol facing remain separate.
AG1146's one visible `B<3:0>` contact represents four scalar formals; grouped
display needs an explicit reversible mapping and a proven target bundle type.
Four ordinary scalar terminals cannot simply share one electrical coordinate.
Six named rounded-gate rail strokes also retain an exact boundary-overshoot
marker; recognition success is not an actual-contact certificate.

| P11. Distinguish a hidden peer from an electrically lone pin. | No-connect marks, hidden names and visible peer labels reflect the complete electrical relation and the selected target convention. | AG1167/1169 native detached-looking resistor crosses; plugin later attaches hidden names. The default shared-well policy is a proposed behavior change. Existing
caller-hidden routing and explicit source omission remain meaningful controls.
`Terminal.hidden` suppresses routing, not caller glyph strokes; these controls
are not interchangeable.
The body study does not give a renderer permission to overrule them.
[Eighteen frozen controls](sixth-bodies.md) keep complete body identities
while varying route participation and no-connect marks. One hidden-class
pair has touching body glyph leads despite zero body routes or labels.
For external body labels, [the fifth study](fifth-bodies.md) separately proves
ten body-to-formal associations through six actual port objects. Text, actual
contact and electrical identity are distinct witnesses. Seven unassociated
free/route labels remain outside that certificate.
The [eighth visibility admission](eighth-bodies-visibility.md) additionally
rejects hidden or transparent source ink that the unchanged coordinate proof
cannot evaluate. The [actual older-plugin round trip](eighth-target.md)
preserves three private-body fixtures through emitted labels; removing a
required peer label changes the exported connection. Appearance and target
electrical identity must retain separate judgments.

Raw membership, accepted ownership and final arrangement are three decisions.
Moving one array earlier in the pipeline does not make them interchangeable.
See [the composition literals](planning.md#group-composition-the-smallest-shared-mechanism-worth-testing)
before adding a shared mechanism.

The [ninth all-MOS census](ninth-local-groups.md) recovers AG1080's five
parents and keeps AG928's single core without using drawing coordinates.
Its eleven raw multigroup candidates are not eleven separate-box admissions:
AG1016 interleaves their extents and AG364 nests output branches around the
inner core. Several other examples, including AG1047, stack stages vertically.
An acyclic gate dependency gives a partial order, not a left-to-right page
direction. Preserve these counterexamples in any arrangement policy.

The [tenth arrangement experiment](tenth-arrangement-plan.md) strengthens that
boundary: AG1028 and AG364 have matching complete interface stars but separated
and nested source arrangements. Keep the incumbent plus bounded H/V proposals;
new nesting remains a separate missing construction. Feasible pose expressions
and lower reported crossings do not establish the promised organization.
Selecting an unchanged flattened incumbent is a fallback, not an improvement.
Ownership uniqueness needs a complete certificate; arrangement optimization
may use an explicitly bounded heuristic after its independent hard gates.

For P13–P18, the original drawings give two concrete freedoms. AG215 and AG1034 have the same six-device differential-load core and assign its crossover to gates or drains. AG1073 and AG1078 give two input pairs the same two drain nets but use side-by-side and nested arrangements. Preserve both branch identities before choosing between those placements. For P19, the useful placement relation depends on what the reader is tracing.
A rail divider driving one MOS gate favors a straight branch from its tap,
even when the resistor bodies are spaced unevenly. A feedback resistor can
occupy either the horizontal return or the output-side leg. A regulator's
output capacitor can stand over its series resistor with neither capacitor
pin on a rail. These qualify `levels.2`, `opamp_grow.2–4` and `forms.10`
respectively. [Examples, counterexamples and selection criteria](../../human-layout-patterns.md#a-divider-that-drives-a-mos-gate)

P19 also applies to passive filters. Put the tapped two-element path of a bridged T on the port row even when a single component directly connects the ports. For P20, choose whether gates or rail pins share a conductor separately from where its bias endpoint or rail lettering goes. For P22, a four-diode connection can appear as individual devices on oblique or cardinal axes, or as a compact functional symbol. The netlist alone cannot establish a physical package or an optional measurement between two terminals. [Filter examples](../../more-human-layout-patterns.md#passive-two-ports) · [Bias and supply examples](../../more-human-layout-patterns.md#bias-ports-and-supply-bars) · [Bridge examples](../../more-human-layout-patterns.md#four-diode-bridges-differential-voltages-and-annotations)

The existing Manhattan safety contract remains binding on current output.
It must be extended coherently rather than deleting assertions to admit a
new route shape. Source direction statistics do not prove an exact numeric
representation, a host capability or a placement selector.

For P19, the [twelfth local-organization trial](twelfth-organization-gate.md)
adds a narrow operational test: five common-control five-MOS parents within
the eleven acyclic multigroup cases support aligned branch rows/columns,
polarity-specific order and directed D/S roles. AG1028 has staggered inputs;
AG1080 has a tail offset from the exact channel midpoint. Neither a universal
alignment rule nor an exact-centering rule survives these sources. The fixed
default-class channel-center bound distinguishes local reconstruction from
translating stretched parents, but it does not certify occupied ink or
routing. Independent clearance, caller and whole-interface gates remain
mandatory; common control is a bounded hypothesis rather than a causal law.

For P20, the [eleventh complementary-stack census](eleventh-partition-candidates.md)
finds six source 2+1 nets in three acyclic parent tuples, plus two continuously
wired nets in a cyclic contrary tuple. This supports a narrow experiment with
complete incidence and caller authority, not a universal drain/gate cut rule
or a causal rule about feedback. Its exact final physical partition, reachable
names and target identity still need production proof. No source three-single
counterpart was found; an explicit caller label request remains authoritative.

No unresolved case is silently converted to a positive or removed from the
denominator. These 27 proposals form an implementation agenda with evidence;
they do not replace the untouched current rule inventory wholesale. The
[amendment ledger](rule-amendments.md) names current rules to retain, narrow
or split and the evidence required before changing their headers. The
[implementation plan](planning.md) supplies stage owners, experiment order,
data/capacity questions and concrete finish lines. The
[implementation packages](implementation-packages.md) make their dependencies
and independent acceptance gates explicit. Subsequent proof results
can narrow or supersede an entry without rewriting its historical evidence.
